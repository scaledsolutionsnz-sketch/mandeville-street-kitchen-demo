Privacy Policy

Mandeville Street Kitchen respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information you share through this website, in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

If you send an enquiry or feedback through this site, we collect the details you provide, typically your name, email, phone number and your message. We don't collect anything you don't choose to send us.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, confirm a booking, or follow up on feedback. We don't sell or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Form handling

Enquiry forms on this site are processed by a third-party form service to deliver your message to us by email. Your details are used solely to pass on that message.

Keeping it secure

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe and only hold it for as long as we need to deal with your enquiry.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by calling +64 21 258 9734.
