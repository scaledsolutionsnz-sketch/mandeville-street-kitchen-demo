Our place

A neighbourhood kitchen, run by people who love feeding people

Mandeville Street Kitchen is a small, independent café in Christchurch built around good coffee, honest food and a relaxed welcome. No fuss, no rush — just a comfortable spot to start your day, catch up with a friend, or get out of the office for a proper break.

Everything's made with care in our own kitchen, and we'd rather know you by name than by table number. Whether you're a daily regular or it's your first visit, you'll get the same warm welcome.

  • Locally owned and run, right here in Christchurch
  • Food made fresh in-house, coffee done properly
  • Friendly, unhurried service — dietary options always sorted
